## USRowing membership

Every TRC athlete must be a USRowing member with a signed waiver to race. [Membership details](https://usrowing.org/sports/2016/6/27/3316_132107070444805064.aspx).

* **Middle school:** Basic membership. It covers general liability and entry to local USRowing-sanctioned regattas.
* **High school:** Championship membership. It adds extra accident coverage and entry to USRowing events such as Regionals and Nationals.

## Missing school

Travel and pre-race practice often mean missing school. Most schools want an excused absence form at the attendance office in advance, and rules differ by school. Wake County families can use the [North Carolina request for excused absence form](https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1753830367/wcpssnet/lxp1pirrg2z7ycicbnwd/reqforexcused-absence.pdf).

## Long-distance regatta departures

* Athletes travel on a bus arranged by TRC and may not drive themselves.
* The bus leaves from and returns to the Bass Pro Shops parking lot, 801 Bass Pro Ln, Cary, NC 27513 ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Bass+Pro+Shops/@35.8303096,-78.773284,17z)).
* You can leave your car overnight, but TRC is not responsible for damage or loss.
* Buses often return late on Saturday or Sunday night. Athletes stay with the team until the coaches say otherwise, and everyone helps unload.
* Departure is usually after school. Estimated arrival times come by email or text.

## Race-day rules

* Athletes attend every regatta unless they give written notice (email is fine). High school athletes should check SAT and ACT dates at the start of each season.
* To race, athletes must attend practice and club events, pay dues and fees on time, and attend required meetings or make arrangements with a coach or the board.
* Coaches choose lineups using erg scores, technique, height and weight, coachability and attendance.
* At local regattas, families provide transportation and arrive on time.
* Athletes stay on team duties until boats are de-rigged and loaded. Families stay in spectator areas, away from the trailer and boat ramp.
* Each athlete brings two labeled bags: one for the bus ride and arrival, and one that is stowed during travel.
