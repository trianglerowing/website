Learn to Row is a summer camp for rising 6th to 12th graders. No rowing experience is needed. Over a week, athletes learn to carry, launch, row and race an eight-person boat with help from coaches and experienced rowers.

## When and where

* Monday to Friday, 9:30 am to 12:30 pm.
* Lake Wheeler Park, 6404 Lake Wheeler Road, Raleigh, NC 27603 ([map](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Triangle+Rowing+Club/@35.6938888,-78.7003007,19z)).

## Swimming requirement

Rowers must be competent swimmers: swim two laps of a 50-yard pool, tread water for 5 minutes, and get out of the deep end without help. Coach boats stay close, but rowers do not wear life jackets in the boats. The club does not give a swim test. Families confirm these skills when they register, because falls from boats are rare but possible.

## A typical week

* **Monday:** mostly on land. Equipment, the rowing machine, stroke technique and rowing from the dock.
* **Tuesday to Thursday:** on the water when the weather allows, with a break around 11:00 and docking at noon.
* **Friday:** race day, with a parent information meeting and 3 or 4 short races.
* Weather can change the plan.

## What to bring

Running shoes that can get wet, two pairs of socks, sunscreen, bug spray, a 32 oz water bottle, a snack, close-fitting athletic clothing (no baggy shorts), a hat and sunglasses.

## Changes, cancellations and refunds

* Ask for a change or cancellation at least 7 days before the session starts.
* Changing sessions costs {{< setting "fees.learn_to_row_session_change" >}}. Canceling more than 7 days before the start costs {{< setting "fees.learn_to_row_cancellation" >}}.
* Registration closes the Wednesday before each session. Incomplete registrations are removed and the place goes to the next family on the waitlist.
* There are no refunds within one week of the start or for weather cancellations.

## What comes next

After camp, families get a free code for fall [Sneak Peek Week](/programs/sneak-peek-week/), a trial week in the competitive season.
