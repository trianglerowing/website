Sneak Peek Week is a one-week trial of in-season practice for students in grades 6 to 12, with or without rowing experience. It is the easiest way to find out whether rowing is for your child. Basic swimming ability is required and is confirmed during registration.

## Schedule

* **High school:** Tuesday to Friday 3:30 to 6:00 pm and Saturday 8:00 to 11:00 am.
* **Middle school girls:** Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday.
* **Middle school boys:** Wednesday, Friday and Saturday.

Practices are at Lake Wheeler Park, 6404 Lake Wheeler Road, Raleigh. See [facilities](/about/facilities/) for parking and pickup.

## What to bring

A hat, sunglasses, running shoes, sunscreen, a water bottle, and close-fitting athletic clothing. Loose clothing can catch in the sliding seat.

After the week, see [how to join](/join/).
