Your child wants to try rowing, or has just joined, and you have never been near a boat. This page is for you. Every rowing word is explained the first time it appears, and the [glossary](/new-to-rowing/glossary/) lists them all in one place.

## What rowing is

Rowing is a team sport on the water. A crew sits in a long, narrow boat called a shell, facing backward, and pulls oars together to move the boat forward. Most crews are one, two, four or eight rowers. Bigger boats also carry a coxswain (said "COCK-sun", often "cox"), a team member who steers and calls instructions but does not row.

Rowers face the back of the boat, so the coxswain or the coach watches where the boat is going. Rowers learn to trust the person steering and to move in rhythm with each other. That is why rowing teaches teamwork, patience and showing up on time.

Triangle Rowing Club (TRC) rows at the boathouse at Lake Wheeler Park, 6404 Lake Wheeler Road, Raleigh. In winter, when the water is too cold, the team trains indoors at Carolina Pines Park. Middle school rowers are in grades 6 to 8. High school rowers are in grades 9 to 12.

## What a week looks like

Rowers come to practice several days a week. Practice has three parts: a warm-up on land, time on the water in boats, and a cool-down. In winter, practice is indoors on rowing machines called ergs (short for ergometer) and on conditioning exercises.

## Your first season at a glance

A rowing year has four parts. The details of dates and costs change, so the questions to the coaches are marked below.

### Fall

The fall season is the starting point for most new rowers. Rowers learn the basics on land and on the water: how to carry a boat, how to sit, and how to take a stroke together. Races in fall are usually head races, which are longer and timed. The [regatta guide](/new-to-rowing/regatta-guide/) explains how they work.

### Winter

When it gets cold, the team moves indoors. Rowers train on ergs, which build fitness and show each rower's progress. A common test is the 2k, a 2,000-meter piece on the erg.

### Spring

Spring is the main racing season. Crews race in sprint races, where boats start side by side and race 2,000 meters. Families usually travel to regattas, so plan for some Saturdays away.
