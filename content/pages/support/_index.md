Triangle Rowing Club is a nonprofit that focuses on youth athletics. We are entirely self-funded, and rowers' fees cover only about half of our costs. Gifts pay for boats, oars, rowing machines, repairs, storage, insurance and tuition reduction for families who need it. Gifts are tax-deductible.

* [Donate](/support/donate/): a one-time gift.
* [Monthly giving](/support/monthly-giving/): a steady gift that replaces much of our piecemeal fundraising.
* [Erg-A-Thon](/support/erg-a-thon/): our yearly fundraiser.
* [Sponsors and employer matching](/support/sponsors/): businesses that support the club, and how an employer can match your gift.
