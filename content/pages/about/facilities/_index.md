TRC practices mainly at Lake Wheeler Park, 6404 Lake Wheeler Road, Raleigh, NC 27603. The park has our boat storage, shelters and fields for water and land training. In winter we train indoors at Carolina Pines Park, 2305 Lake Wheeler Road, Raleigh, NC 27603. Check the [Lake Wheeler weather forecast](https://forecast7.com/en/35d66n78d68/27603/?unit=us).

## Park rules

Lake Wheeler is a City of Raleigh park and the only gated park in the city's system. TRC negotiated access to the lake, so everyone must follow the park's rules and leave by closing time so staff can lock up.

* The speed limit is 15 mph. The park has many walkers, children and residents, wooded blind spots, and police patrols that give speeding tickets.

## Parking and pickup

* TRC vehicles may not use the lot nearest the waterfront.
* Athletes and parents may use the athlete lot by the playground if spaces are open. There is no carpool line.
* If the athlete lot is full, use the overflow lot on the map. Do not park on the gravel shoulder across from the athlete lot, marked with red X's.
* On Saturdays, everyone parks in the field lot to reduce weekend public traffic.
* Follow these rules even if someone told you otherwise. We set them with the lake staff.
