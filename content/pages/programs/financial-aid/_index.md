TRC offers need-based financial aid so that cost does not keep a child from rowing. The program is called tuition reduction, or (TR)2.

## Who can apply

Any TRC rowing family can apply, including families new to rowing. Awards cover 10% to 75% of a season's fees and are based mainly on financial need. The committee also considers coaches' comments on attitude, commitment and participation. Parents or guardians of recipients must meet the normal [volunteer requirement](/families/volunteering/). The athlete must already meet the swimming requirement.

## Deadlines

Aid is offered twice a year, for the fall and winter seasons and for the spring and summer seasons.

## How to apply

1. Fill out the [tuition reduction application form]({{< setting "links.tuition_reduction_application" >}}) completely, even if you applied before.
2. Include a short statement or essay about your family's financial need. Other documents are optional and kept private.
3. Wait for the decision before you register for the next season in SportsEngine. Approved families get a discount code to use when they register.

Only the TRC Financial Assistance Committee reads applications, and the review is confidential.
