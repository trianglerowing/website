Triangle Rowing Club (TRC) is a nonprofit rowing club for middle and high school students in Wake County, North Carolina. We practice at Lake Wheeler Park in Raleigh. Beginners are welcome: you do not need to have rowed before. Our rowers go on to race at regional and national regattas, and many row in college.

## Start here

* Your child is curious about rowing: [tell us you are interested](/join/), then read the [guide for parents new to rowing](/new-to-rowing/).
* You want prices and schedules: see [programs and costs](/programs/).
* Your child already rows with us: go to the [families page](/families/) for the calendar, practice times and volunteering.

## Support the club

TRC is entirely self-funded, and rowers' fees cover only about half of the club's costs. [Ways to give](/support/) include one-time gifts, monthly giving and the Erg-A-Thon, and employers can match gifts. Thank you to our [sponsors](/support/sponsors/).

## Our home waters

We practice at Lake Wheeler Park, a City of Raleigh park. Read more about our [facilities](/about/facilities/). Adults who want to row can contact the [Raleigh Rowing Center](https://raleighrowing.org/), because TRC has no adult program. College rowers can look at the [Rowing Club at NC State](https://rowingclubncsu.com/).

Questions? Email [info@trianglerowing.org](mailto:info@trianglerowing.org).
