* **46** boats have qualified for the USRowing Youth National Championships.
* **4** members of the US Junior National Team.
* TRC alumni have rowed at 45 colleges (list below).

## Recent results

* **October 2025:** A team record of 22 athletes raced at the Head of the Charles in five events and earned three guaranteed entries for 2026.
* **July 2025:** A 2025 graduate rowed bow in the USA mixed quad at the Rhine-Ruhr World University Games and won the B final.
* **June 2025:** At Youth Nationals in Florida, TRC set team bests in four events and had eight finishes in the top 27.
* **May 2025:** Eight boats qualified for Youth Nationals from the Southeast Regionals. Three crews won silver, and the men's U17 eight won bronze.
* **April 2025:** At the Dogwood Junior Championships, TRC won the most medals in its history.
* **November 2024:** The varsity men won their first gold at the Head of the South and set a club record in the men's four at the Head of the Charles.
* **September 2024:** USRowing named a TRC rower its Beach Sprint Female Athlete of the Year.
* **June 2024:** A TRC rower won the women's single national title at Youth Nationals.
* **June 2022:** TRC won two national titles at Youth Nationals for the first time, in the men's youth pair and the women's U17 single.
