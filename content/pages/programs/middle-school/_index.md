Our middle school program is for students in grades 6 to 8. It builds fitness, teamwork, boat handling and responsibility, and it aims to grow long-term athletes. No experience is needed. Rowers need basic swimming ability and a valid sports physical. Middle schoolers train alongside the high school squads.

## Schedule

* **Girls:** Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday.
* **Boys:** Wednesday, Friday and Saturday.
* Weekdays 3:30 to 6:00 pm, Saturdays 8:00 to 11:00 am. Practice ends earlier in November and February.
* That is 2 to 3 practices a week, depending on the season. See the [training schedule](/families/practice/) and the [calendar](/families/calendar/).

## Seasons

* **Fall:** {{< setting "seasons.fall" >}}. Races are 3,000 to 5,000 meters, and crews race the clock.
* **Spring:** {{< setting "seasons.spring" >}}. Races are 1,500 to 2,000 meters, with boats racing side by side.
* **Summer and winter:** optional [off-season training](/programs/off-season-training/).

Each competitive season includes two regattas in North Carolina, in High Point and Elizabeth City. See the [regatta schedule](/families/regatta-schedule/).

## Cost

* Season dues: {{< setting "fees.middle_school_dues" >}}, which cover equipment, racing, food and coaching.
* A one-time uniform purchase. See the [gear page](/new-to-rowing/gear/).
* Off-season training: {{< setting "fees.off_season_middle_school" >}}.
* Payment plans, scholarships and [tuition reduction](/programs/financial-aid/) are available.

## Physical form

Every rower needs a sports physical, valid for one year, uploaded in SportsEngine at the start of each season. The doctor can complete the [TRC Medical Practitioner Clearance Form](/documents/medical-practitioner-clearance-form.pdf).

## Join

Try rowing first at [Sneak Peek Week](/programs/sneak-peek-week/) or a summer [Learn to Row](/programs/learn-to-row/) camp. Then follow the [steps to join](/join/). Questions: [info@trianglerowing.org](mailto:info@trianglerowing.org). Athletes with rowing experience can write to [coach@trianglerowing.org](mailto:coach@trianglerowing.org).
