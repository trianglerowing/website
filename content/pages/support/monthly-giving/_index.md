A Sustainer is anyone who gives to TRC every month: family, alumni, neighbors, professionals or rowers. The gift is automatic and tax-deductible. You choose the amount and whether it goes to the general operating fund or to scholarships.

Monthly gifts pay for equipment: replacing aging gear, repairs and new purchases. They also reduce how much the club has to fundraise, so more time goes to the rowers.

## How to sign up

1. Open the donation page with the button below.
2. Choose Monthly, or another repeat period, and the amount.
3. Complete payment by debit or credit card, PayPal, Venmo, Google Pay or Apple Pay.

## Questions

**What is the Sustainer program?** A monthly giving program for the club's equipment budget. With more than 100 rowers, the goal is an average of {{< setting "fundraising.sustainer_goal_per_rower_per_month" >}} per rower per month, or {{< setting "fundraising.sustainer_annual_goal" >}} a year, for equipment and other club costs.

**Why does TRC need it?** TRC is self-funded. As the club grows, it must keep upgrading boats and equipment to stay competitive, and it pays for storage, insurance and administration. Typical prices:

* An eight-person boat: $30,000 to $40,000 new, or $15,000 to $20,000 used.
* A set of 8 oars: more than $3,400.
* A new rowing machine (erg): more than $900, or $400 to $600 used.
* A coxswain's voice amplifier: close to $600.
* Coach boats and outboard motors need regular repair and replacement.

**What does a Sustainer get?** Steady funding for the club, a tax deduction, automatic payments with no monthly checks, and the choice of general or scholarship fund.

**Does it replace other fundraising?** No. It gives a reliable base so the club needs less piecemeal fundraising, and it sits alongside the Sponsor a Rower program and one-time gifts.
