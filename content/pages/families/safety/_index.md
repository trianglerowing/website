## First aid and the water

* First aid kits and automated external defibrillators (AEDs) are on site at Lake Wheeler.
* Coaches are CPR certified.
* Motorized coach boats stay within a safe distance of the team and carry life jackets for athletes.
* Tell a coach right away about any injury or accident.

## SafeSport training

The U.S. Center for SafeSport works to end sexual, physical and emotional abuse in amateur sports. TRC encourages every adult and volunteer to be certified, and some volunteers must be before they start.

1. Certified training takes about three hours and can be split across sessions.
2. Follow the [SafeSport training instructions](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Zk7hgysRBNTvUa7ZTcHaaLGtoUwBfoRqLceQsn5sfVU/edit?usp=sharing) to create a USRowing Supporter account and take the course.
3. If you were certified before, you still need the new account. Your earlier training should link to it if your name and birthdate match.
4. If your training has expired, or will expire this season, sign in to your USRowing account and take the refresher. Yearly upkeep takes under an hour.
5. Questions: [safety@trianglerowing.org](mailto:safety@trianglerowing.org?subject=SafeSport%20Question%2FIssue), the TRC Safety Coordinator.

## Background checks

Any volunteer who has contact with athletes must pass a background check. The National Center for Safety Initiatives (NCSI), a USRowing partner, runs the checks. NCSI tells only TRC's officers whether a result is clear or not clear.

* A check is valid for two years. If you are not sure yours is still valid, email [secretary@trianglerowing.org](mailto:secretary@trianglerowing.org?subject=background%20check%20question).
