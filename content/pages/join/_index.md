TRC is a competitive rowing team for students in grades 6 to 12. Beginners are welcome. We aim to take a new rower to a national-level athlete.

## Three steps

1. **Tell us you are interested.** We will send information about getting started and upcoming chances to try rowing.

   {{< register program="interest" >}}

2. **Try rowing.** No experience is needed. Come to a summer [Learn to Row](/programs/learn-to-row/) camp or a fall [Sneak Peek Week](/programs/sneak-peek-week/). Bring workout clothes, water and a willingness to work.
3. **Register and get gear.** When your child commits, we guide you through online registration, [uniform ordering](/new-to-rowing/gear/), season dues, [financial aid](/programs/financial-aid/) and the USRowing membership and waiver. Find costs on the [programs page](/programs/) and registration tips on the [registration help page](/families/registration-help/).

## Who can join

* Students in grades 6 to 12.
* No rowing experience needed.
* Basic swimming ability.
* Willingness to commit, attend practice and give full effort.

## What we expect

Rowing is a team sport with a competitive program. Athletes attend practice regularly, push themselves and their teammates, balance school and training, race at local and travel regattas, and have fun.

## Questions

Email the coaching staff at [coach@trianglerowing.org](mailto:coach@trianglerowing.org) or the club at [info@trianglerowing.org](mailto:info@trianglerowing.org). New parents can start with the [guide for parents new to rowing](/new-to-rowing/).
