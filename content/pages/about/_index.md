Triangle Rowing Club (TRC) is a nonprofit rowing team for middle and high school students in Wake County, North Carolina. We have been a 501(c)(3) charity since 2008, with more than 100 rowers from many area schools. Our mission is developing character and leadership through team sport. We are self-funded, and rowers' fees cover about half of our costs.

TRC rows at Lake Wheeler Park, a City of Raleigh park, and trains indoors at Carolina Pines Park in winter. We work with the City of Raleigh Parks, Recreation and Cultural Resources department.
