Short answers for parents of new rowers. Words you may not know are explained in the [glossary](/new-to-rowing/glossary/).

## Does my child need rowing experience?

No. Most rowers at TRC start with no experience. Coaches teach the basics on land first, then in the boat.

## Does size or strength matter?

Rowing has a place for every body type. Taller rowers have a longer reach, but every boat needs people who work well together. What matters most is effort and showing up. A coxswain, the teammate who steers and calls the race, can be small or large, and does not row.

## What if my child misses practice?

Rowing is a team sport. A crew of eight cannot row well if two seats are empty, so coaches ask rowers to tell them ahead of time when they will miss a practice.

## What does rowing cost?

Costs fall into three groups: dues for the season, fees for each regatta, and gear and uniform. Dues are different for middle school and high school. TRC also offers help with dues.
