TRC is run by families. Each overnight regatta needs roughly 85 to 100 parent hours at the regatta site, and volunteering keeps costs down for everyone.

## How it works

Volunteer jobs earn tokens. The number of tokens for each job is listed in the sign-up sheet for each regatta.

Requirement for each high school rower: {{< setting "volunteer.tokens_per_high_school_rower" >}}

* Families with more than one high school rower owe the requirement for each rower. You can mix season-long, year-long, regatta-long and single-shift jobs.
* If you do a season-long, year-long or regatta-long job, you do not also count tokens.
* There is no partial credit for part of the requirement.

## If you cannot meet the requirement

You pay a volunteer opt-out fee of {{< setting "fees.volunteer_opt_out" >}}. The fee is not pro-rated, is not refundable, and replaces your volunteer requirement. Families who have not met the requirement are invoiced automatically after the last regatta. Until tokens or the fee are paid, the athlete cannot take part in future events. To talk about options, email the [Volunteer Coordinator](mailto:volunteercoordinator@trianglerowing.org?subject=Volunteer%20Options%20Discussion).

## Common questions

* **What if my skills do not match an open job?** Email the Volunteer Coordinator, who will find a fit.
* **Are the jobs hard?** Most complex roles are held long term by experienced parents. Grill master is learned over a few sessions, and the chuck wagon needs a large vehicle with a hitch and towing experience. Any other job you can sign up for, and an experienced volunteer will show you what to do.
* **How do I pick a shift if I do not know my rower's race time?** You sign up before race times are known. You are never expected to miss your child's race. Step away for the race, then return to your post.
* **Can middle school parents volunteer?** Yes. Email the Volunteer Coordinator.
* **Do all regattas need volunteers?** No. Head of the Charles in fall and Youth Nationals in spring do not. Head of the Hooch in fall and the USRowing Southeast Regionals in spring (when not in Sarasota) usually do, depending on the coaches' choices.
* **Where is SafeSport training?** On the [safety page](/families/safety/).
