## Employer matching

Many Triangle employers match their employees' gifts to charities. Some also match volunteer hours, or gifts from retirees, spouses and surviving spouses. TRC is a qualified nonprofit and is eligible for most matching gift programs.

1. Ask your human resources department whether your company has a matching program, and what proof of the gift it needs.
2. Complete your employer's matching form and attach your gift receipt.
3. For help, write to the [Fundraising Coordinator](mailto:fundraiser@trianglerowing.org).

Companies that offer matching include Cisco, Red Hat, Oracle, Progress Energy and SAS.
