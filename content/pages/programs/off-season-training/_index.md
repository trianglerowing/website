Off-season training helps athletes improve fitness, flexibility, erg times and technique. It is open to all TRC athletes and recommended, but not required.

## Summer rowing

For current members who want to keep rowing over the summer.

* **High school:** four mornings a week, Monday to Thursday, 8:30 to 10:30 am.
* **Middle school:** two mornings a week, Tuesday and Thursday, 8:30 to 10:30 am. Middle schoolers need to have finished one season of rowing first.
* Rising 9th graders may choose either schedule.
* Summer has two sessions (June to July, and July to August). Athletes can sign up for both.
* Cost per session: {{< setting "fees.summer_training_middle_school" >}} for middle school and {{< setting "fees.summer_training_high_school" >}} for high school.

## Winter training

Winter training is the base for a strong spring. Most of it is indoors on rowing machines (ergs), with strength and conditioning.

* **High school:** Monday, Wednesday and Friday 3:30 to 6:00 pm. Athletes in the three-day group can add Tuesday and Thursday for five days a week.
* **Middle school:** Tuesday and Thursday 3:45 to 6:00 pm, with at least half of the time on the erg.
* Training takes maturity, self-direction and hard work.
* Season dates and the typical cost range: {{< setting "fees.off_season_high_school" >}} for high school and {{< setting "fees.off_season_middle_school" >}} for middle school.
