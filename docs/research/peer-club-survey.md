## 2. Atlanta Junior Rowing Association (AJRA): full review

Site: https://www.atlantajuniors.com. Nonprofit 501(c)(3), founded 1988, Roswell GA, 200+ rowers from 75+ schools, largest GA program. Not to be confused with Atlanta Rowing Club (atlantarow.org, SportsEngine, adult/masters).

Platform stack: Wix site, SportsEngine for registration (sportsengine.com/org/atlanta-junior-rowing-association), Zeffy (free-for-nonprofit payment/donation tool) for prepay, Square site for spiritwear, Instagram/Facebook/YouTube.

Navigation: Inside AJRA (About Us, Our Coaches, Our Board) | Our Programs (Competitive, Developmental, Recreational, Winter Training, Summer Camps) | Become a Sponsor | FAQs | Parent Portal | Connect (Calendar, News, Contact, From Blade to Buoy). Persistent "Join Now" button in header.

Homepage: four feature cards (Welcome, Our Programs, Happening Now/Calendar, Our Achievements/News), seasonal banner ("Winter Training registration NOW OPEN"), upcoming-season buttons per program tier, results highlight, linked sponsor logo strip, mission line, address and phone in footer.

Pages reviewed and what they do well:
- Programs (/our-programs): a tier table by audience (Developmental = grades 7-8, Competitive = HS Novice/Varsity, Recreational = HS low-commitment, Summer Camps, Winter Training). Tiering by commitment level is the main structural idea to copy: it answers "which one is for my kid".
- FAQs (/faqs): 14 questions, all-caps question then short paragraph. Strong answers: no experience needed; no ideal size, small kids often cox; where should a no-experience high schooler start (Competitive Novice, Recreational as fallback); no trial lessons but first two weeks act as trial with partial refund; what to wear (fitted clothing, no Crocs); what season dues cover (race entries, coaching, hotels, bus, meals); $100 non-refundable deposit and payment plan; absence policy (3+ unexcused affects boat placement); code of conduct signed at registration; "can a kid at a rigorous school handle this" answer; carpool roster. Gaps: no dollar amounts, no financial aid, no volunteering.
- Program pages (/developmental, /recreational, /competitive): each has a dated registration banner, exact season dates, practice times per squad, price, what is included, regatta list with dates and city, and a closing exhibition for family (Roswell Rumble). Competitive page: $2,800, six days a week, tentative regatta list (American Youth Cup, Shamrock Sprints, Dogwood Junior Championships, Southeast Regionals, Youth Nationals), extra-fee events flagged. Developmental $700 weekday / $475 Saturday; Recreational $795. Visible pricing is a clear win. Defects: typo "12:30 am", stale "fall season" text on a spring page, no explanation of what the listed regattas are.
- Winter Training (/winter-training): framed as "The Season Between The Seasons"; separate schedules by gender/group; $400/$200; three-step registration. Defect: confusing double-system flow (Zeffy prepay, discount code by email, then SportsEngine); Zeffy donation prompt must be dismissed; middle school links point at the wrong forms.
- Calendar (/calendar): one-line intro "click the event for details and save it to your calendar"; embedded calendar (type not detectable from fetch).
- Sponsor (/sponsor): concrete sponsor benefit list (logo on site, newsletter, banner, shirt, food trailer reaching 50,000 regatta attendees) and "what your gift buys" amounts: $2,200 = one athlete season, $2,350 = a regatta day of team meals, $1,500 = team shirts, $200/seat to $55,000/8+ shell. Tier chart is an image (not accessible/searchable). No volunteer asks.
- Coaches (/our-coaches): headshot, title, narrative bio, email, personal philosophy; Level II credentials named for some; four bios are blank placeholders; no SafeSport or background-check statement.
- About (/about-us): mission (character, commitment, community), history, size. No aid, SafeSport or college outcomes text.
- Parent Portal (/parent-portal): code of conduct and handbook sit behind login, so not crawlable; names exist but content cannot be assessed. Parent meeting after week one gives racing schedule.
- Not found on the public site: new-parent guide, regatta explainer, glossary, volunteer page, fundraising page beyond sponsorship, financial aid, SafeSport page, college pathways page.

What TRC should copy from AJRA:
1. The tier-by-commitment programs table with grade range, days/times, season dates and price on one page.
2. Published prices plus "what the dues cover".
3. FAQ phrasing for fears: no experience, body size, coxswain role, trial period, rigorous schools, absence policy.
4. Persistent Join Now button and seasonal "registration open" banner.
5. "What your gift buys" donation framing and a sponsor benefits list.
6. A family-invited season-ending event listed on the program page.

What TRC should do better than AJRA: add the missing regatta explainer, glossary, volunteer page, financial aid, SafeSport and coach credentials; avoid putting handbooks behind a login; give one registration flow instead of Zeffy plus SportsEngine; publish an accessible (text) sponsor tier table; fix date and copy errors.

Cross-club norms worth stating on TRC's site (what peers publish)
- Time commitment: novice 3 days/week at Charlotte, 3 weekday practices at AJRA developmental, 4-5 at Justice, six days for competitive teams.
- Cost range: $475 to $2,800 per season (AJRA), $1,400-1,800 (Charlotte). Travel usually separate for parents.
- Attendance rules: 80-85% minimum at Charlotte and CRI, 3 unexcused absences at AJRA.
- Tools: Regatta Central (entries, results, SafeSport check), CrewTimer, GroupMe, Heja, SportsEngine, SportsYou, iCrew.

## 4. Recommended TRC page list, ranked by parent value

1. "New to rowing? Start here" parent hub: what rowing is, what your child does week to week, what a first season looks like, who to call. Plus one-page "first season at a glance" timeline (fall, winter, spring, summer). Models: FHC, Justice.
2. Programs and pricing by level: grade range, schedule, season dates, price, what dues cover, what is extra, one Join button. Model: AJRA.
3. Parent FAQ (new-parent questions): no experience, size/fitness, swim test, trial period, school balance, absences, refunds, safety. Model: AJRA FAQ, Charlotte FAQ.
4. Regatta guide for spectators and families: what a regatta is, fall head races vs spring sprints, how to find the heat sheet on Regatta Central and results on CrewTimer, race-day timeline, what to bring (parent and rower lists), parent tent, parking, travel costs. Models: HB Crew, FHC, Norwalk, Williamsburg.
5. Financial aid and costs page: how to apply, who qualifies, what aid covers, hidden costs (travel, gear, nationals). Models: CRI, VIRC, Williamsburg; none publish it well.
6. Glossary and boat classes: coxswain, sweep vs sculling, erg, 2k, bow/stroke, 1x/2x/4x/4+/8+, novice vs varsity. Illustrations. Model: Sarasota (as concept), HB Crew inline.
7. Gear and what to buy/wear: required vs loaner vs optional, uniform, team store timing. Models: VIRC, AJRA.
8. Safety and SafeSport: required courses with steps, coach screening, weather/lightning, swim test, incident reporting. Models: VIRC, USRowing.
9. Parent communication and tools: which apps, who to contact, calendar subscribe, absence process. Model: Northampton, ORRA.
10. Volunteer and parent association page: roles, hours, sign-up, food tent routine. Models: Justice, Northampton, FHC.
11. Calendar with season structure: filterable by program, regatta types labeled, links to Regatta Central entries. Model: AJRA, FHC.
12. Coaches page: bio, credentials, certifications, SafeSport status, photo. Model: AJRA (fixed).
13. Donate, sponsors, fundraising: "what your gift buys", sponsor benefit list in text, tax-deductible note. Model: AJRA sponsor page, FHC.
14. Rowing 101 explainer series: the boat, the stroke, the race (short pages or illustrated sections). Model: FHC.
15. College and recruiting pathways: what clubs offer, timeline, NCAA/NCSA basics, alumni placements. Peers are thin here; low cost differentiator.
16. Alumni and results/news: season results, where alumni row. Model: AJRA news and Collegiate Record.

Design notes: audience-grouped menu (Parents, Programs, Support); sticky Join/Get started button; seasonal registration banner; fully public parent content (no login wall); plain URLs; mobile-first, text-based tables; avoid image-only charts; link out to Regatta Central, CrewTimer and USRowing SafeSport rather than copying their content.
