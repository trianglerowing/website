## Summer

Summer sessions for experienced athletes run June to August. [Learn to Row](/programs/learn-to-row/) camps for new rowers run in the summer too.

## Where

Boats are stored at Lake Wheeler Park, 6404 Lake Wheeler Road, Raleigh ([directions](https://maps.app.goo.gl/k4qfkEE1hy1d5rVp6)). See [facilities](/about/facilities/) for parking.

## Pickup

Pick your athlete up 30 minutes before sunset, whatever the schedule says. Practice can run a little late because of group talks and putting boats away, so bring something to do while you wait.

## Attendance

Athletes are expected at every practice. An 8-seat boat cannot row with 7 rowers, so one missing rower can change the plan for the whole boat.

* Tell your coach about unavoidable conflicts at the start of the season, or as soon as you know.
* Emergencies, including fast-developing illness, are excused.

## Weather

Rowing is outdoors, and safety comes first. Coaches send updates about weather that affects practices, travel and regattas, and tell athletes and parents about schedule changes as soon as possible.

## Illness

The athlete tells the coach about a missed practice, ideally by text as early as possible. If your child is too sick to text, a parent should contact the coach.

More answers are in the [Parent FAQ](/new-to-rowing/parent-faq/).
