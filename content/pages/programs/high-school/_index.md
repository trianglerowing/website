Our high school program has men's and women's novice and varsity crews for students in grades 9 to 12. No experience is needed. Rowers need basic swimming ability and a valid sports physical.

## Schedule

* In season, rowers practice 4 to 6 times a week.
* Weekdays 3:30 to 6:00 pm and Saturdays 8:00 to 11:00 am. Monday practice is for varsity only.
* Practice ends earlier in November and February because the sun sets earlier.
* The full [training schedule](/families/practice/) and the [calendar](/families/calendar/) have the details.

## Seasons

* **Fall:** {{< setting "seasons.fall" >}}. Races are head races of about 5,000 meters, where boats start one at a time and race the clock. Crews attend three regattas, and some are invited to the Head of the Hooch and the Head of the Charles.
* **Spring:** {{< setting "seasons.spring" >}}. Races are sprints of about 1,500 to 2,000 meters. Crews attend three regattas, and some qualify for Regionals and Nationals.
* **Summer and winter:** optional [off-season training](/programs/off-season-training/).

See the [regatta schedule](/families/regatta-schedule/) and our [achievements](/about/achievements/).

## Physical form

Every rower needs a sports physical, valid for one year, uploaded in SportsEngine at the start of each season. The doctor can complete the [TRC Medical Practitioner Clearance Form](/documents/medical-practitioner-clearance-form.pdf).

## Join

Try rowing first at [Sneak Peek Week](/programs/sneak-peek-week/) or a summer [Learn to Row](/programs/learn-to-row/) camp. Then follow the [steps to join](/join/). Questions: [info@trianglerowing.org](mailto:info@trianglerowing.org). Athletes with rowing experience can write to [coach@trianglerowing.org](mailto:coach@trianglerowing.org).
