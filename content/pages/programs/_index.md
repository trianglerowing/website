TRC offers year-round rowing for students in grades 6 to 12. Beginners are welcome in every program. We do not run an adult program. Adults can contact the [Raleigh Rowing Center](https://raleighrowing.org/).

## What the dues cover

Season dues cover equipment, racing, coaching and food on regatta days. Middle school rowers also make a one-time uniform purchase. Travel regattas and uniforms can cost extra; see the [regatta schedule](/families/regatta-schedule/) and the [gear page](/new-to-rowing/gear/).

## Ways to pay less

Payment plans, scholarships and need-based [tuition reduction](/programs/financial-aid/) are available. Volunteer time is also part of every high school family's season; see [volunteering](/families/volunteering/).

## The two squads

* **High school:** men's and women's novice and varsity crews. The season runs from fall head races to spring championship regattas. The fastest crews can qualify for the USRowing Youth National Championships.
* **Middle school:** a co-ed program built around fitness, teamwork and responsibility. Middle schoolers race in North Carolina and often train alongside high school rowers.

## How to start

Not sure yet? Come to [Sneak Peek Week](/programs/sneak-peek-week/) or a summer [Learn to Row](/programs/learn-to-row/) camp, or read the [guide for parents](/new-to-rowing/). Ready to go? See [how to join](/join/).
