## What to bring

* Layers that fit the weather. Shirts stay on at every practice.
* No cotton. It stays wet and cold.
* Sunscreen.
* Running shoes for land workouts.
* Slides or old shoes for the dock. They get wet and muddy.
* Plain water bottle, full. Plastic is best; a metal bottle can dent a boat.
* Sunglasses and a visor or cap.
* Socks for the shoes fixed in the boat.

## Fuel and water

* Eat a snack about an hour before practice: fruit, pretzels, yogurt or nuts.
* Lunch is not a snack.
* Drink all day. Pale urine means you are hydrated.

## Safety on the water

* Carry boats with the right number of people and a coxswain.
* Tell a coach about damaged gear right away.
* Stretch and foam roll after practice.

## Uniforms

* **High school:** a TRC uni-suit, a TRC tech shirt and a TRC navy travel shirt (V-neck or crew neck).
* **Middle school:** black or navy compression shorts, which TRC does not sell, and an orange TRC team T-shirt.
* Every rower's torso and chest must be covered at all times. If the uni-suit rides up, wear a top over it. High school rowers can buy the middle school T-shirt for this.

[Buy uniforms at the TRC Square store](https://trianglerowing.square.site/)

## Team jacket

Jackets are not required and are not part of the uniform. The Boat House Gore-Tex jacket is warm and waterproof.

* Jackets are custom-ordered and sold once a year, in the fall.
* The order goes to the maker only if at least 6 jackets are ordered.
* Delivery can take up to 6 weeks.

## Spirit wear

T-shirts, hoodies, sweatpants, rain jackets and more are sold through the Squadlocker store, and orders ship to your home. The store is open all the time. Magnets, stickers and other logo items help support the team.

[Order spirit wear from Squadlocker](https://teamlocker.squadlocker.com/#/lockers/triangle-rowing-club-1913870)
