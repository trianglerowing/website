Registration, payment and your family profile are handled in SportsEngine, the service the club uses for rosters and payments. This website links to it and does not store your information.

[Sign in to SportsEngine]({{< setting "links.member_login" >}})

## Before you register

The first registration takes longer than later ones. Gather this first:

1. The athlete's basic information, email address and phone number.
2. An emergency contact who is not a parent or guardian, with contact details.
3. The name and contact details of the athlete's medical practitioner, and the preferred hospital.
4. A credit card for payment.
5. The athlete's health history, with details of any issues.
6. The date of the athlete's last physical.
7. A current medical insurance card, scanned on the front and back, ready to upload.
8. Confirmation that the athlete can swim 100 yards (four laps of a 25-yard pool) without touching the bottom and tread water for 5 minutes.
9. The participation form signed and dated by a medical practitioner. You do not need it to finish registering, but it must be uploaded before the athlete can practice. Download the [TRC Medical Practitioner Clearance Form](/documents/medical-practitioner-clearance-form.pdf).

## Changes after you register

In SportsEngine, sign in with the account you registered with, choose your profile and settings, then My SportsEngine.

* **Change a registration or upload forms:** choose Registrations, pick the registration, choose the pencil icon beside the section, make your change and choose Save Registration Entry.
* **Update phone, email or other details:** choose Household, then View Profile for the person, and edit Profile Information.
* **Add another parent or guardian:** choose Household, then View Profile for the rower, choose the plus sign next to Guardians and send an invitation to the new guardian's email.

See the [programs and costs page](/programs/). For help paying, see [financial aid](/programs/financial-aid/). Approved families receive a discount code to use at registration.
